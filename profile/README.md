## First Look at Sync2

Sync2 is for people who keep Outlook on two PCs they own and want one named mailbox bridge. It gives you a calendar desk, own folders, and a short path back to the pair you already run. It does not replace Outlook. It sits beside Outlook and keeps the folders close.

Work stays on PCs and folders you own. Extra machines wait for their own title.
